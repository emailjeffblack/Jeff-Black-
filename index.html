More than a résumé.

I believe some of the best lessons in life come from building, fixing, trying, failing, learning—and trying again.

I was born in Logan, Utah while my dad was going to college to be a school teacher. I love helping people and it has become my lifelong passion. I very much enjoy fixing things and this is what I do both for most senarios in life. If you look long and hard enough there is always a solution to each difficulty you come across.

I remember living in Logan even though I was only 4 years old when we moved away. We moved around a lot when I was a young lad. By the time I was 18 years we had moved a lot, so this was definately something I had to get used to. I have fond memories of some of my school teachers and not so fond memories of others. I learned to be shy early on because the new kid in school is always the one who gets picked on. Just because I was shy didn't have to define my life and I have since learned to love people and to help them as much as I can.

02 / LIFE EXPERIENCES

Chapters worth remembering.

CHAPTER 01

Where It Started

My first adverse experience that has defined who I am is an experience that happened to me in second grade that I ahall never forget. My dad was a school teacher at the school and some of his students had become upset at him for an unknown reason, At recess time I was the last student to leave the classroom, the teacher was already gone somewhere and several larger students came in and threw a blanket over me. The students then hit and punched me untill I was bruised all over. Then they took the blanket and left. I was too scared to tell my dad or anyone and kept this as a life lesson that the world was different that what I had previously imagined. There is a life long positive that I have taken from this experience. I have a lifelong desire to protect others from bullies and have instilled this into my children. It helped them know when abuse came their way, this was not okay and they were able to in the end to escape the abuse and return back to me.

CHAPTER 02

Learning by Doing

I learned to fix things by trial and error and the instructions of my dad or just by doing. It really began as a curiosity, at 10 years old I remember getting into trouble because I was out in the driveway jacking up cars with an old jack that I had found. This has created a lifelong desire to fix things and it started with cars and trucks. I learned first by fixing and servicing my own vehicles.

CHAPTER 03

A Turning Point

I have had several turning points in my life. I think the one that most stands out is when the Church, under Warren Jeffs, decided to send me away. My wife and I were put in an impossible situation that is hard to explain and My children were taken away from me. For 2 years I struggled believing that I was doing all in my power to get back to them. This turned out to be very deceptive because after nearly 2 years my oldest daughter called me and leterally fled in the nighttime to bring my children to me and I found out they had been horribly abused, much to my shock and dispair. It has been nearly 10 years now since I got them back and everyday we heal a little and get a little stronger.

CHAPTER 04

Still Exploring

I am still fixing cars and trucks, although I'm a bit slower at it than I used to be. I enjoy repairing vacuums and other household items. I am still actively presuing my automotive knowledge and diagnostics. My dream is to travel with my lovely wife of over 30 years and experience life together in different places. I love to learn and have taken the Techmoms class and am now a Techdad. I love to learn and will continue to learn as opportunity and desire meet to continue my education continually.
