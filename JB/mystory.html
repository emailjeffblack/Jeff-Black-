My Story

A life built by doing. The people, places, challenges and adventures that made me who I am.

Where It All Began

I was born in Logan, UT in the during the winter of 1971. My dad was going to college at the time to become a schoolteacher. He majored in industrial arts mainly in Auto Mechanics. This is important as it helped to shape my life

This story that I am writing here is only meant to be just a small snapshot of my life and it is not intended to replace the whole story which I hope I can complete someday.

My family moved a lot. From Logan, UT we moved to Cedar City, UT and lived there for about a year as I turned 5 and beyond. We then moved to Hildale, Utah where I went to first grade and then we moved to Colorado City, AZ where I want to second grade consequently the first and second grade school was in Colorado City, AZ as Hildale, UT didn't have a school at that time. After second grade we moved to Washington, UT where I went to third grade for about 3 months then we moved to The Valley of Fire near Overton, NV where I went to more of Third Grade for about 3 months. My teachere here was a MR. Jones and he was always yelling at the students and I felt like he took an especial dislike of me. I quickly became so shy that I was put in a really fun class that I looked fowrard to each day. I found out later in Life that this "fun class" was in fact special Eduction. After the before mentioned 3 months then we moved back to Washington, UT where I finished out the rest of the year of the Third Grade.This kept me on my toes by always being the "New Kid" in school It made it more difficult to make friends and I believe it contributed a lot to my shyness that haunted me even as an adult.

From there we moved to West Valley City, UT where I attended 4th grade. This is where I became an honor student and learned to put potatoe chips in my sandwiches from my teacher. For Fifth Grade we had moved to Magna, UT. Here I experienced one of the many bullies I encounterd at most of the many schools I attended. I do not remember his name; I do remember him beating me up several times because I stood up to him when he was picking on some of the other students The next day he became my friend and I was able to curb his appetite to pick on others by suggesting I may stand up to him again. Aparently he very much disliked this as he was a large student and no one had aparently stood up to him before.

For Sixth grade we had moved again and this time I went to School in Sandy, UT at a place called "Mount Jorday Middle School". At this school I had my first experience of having multiple classrooms. I learned a lot and enjoyed school, although I didn't enjoy the bullies and there were many, including the ones that took pleasure in throwing rocks at myself and older sister as we walked to and from school. This helped to shape my mentality and continuous desire to always be polite and treat people good as I knew so much of the opposite growing up.

Seventh Grade found me as one of the many students of my Dad. We had moved to Buckhorn Ranch which was North of Cedar City and North of Parowan, UT and South of Bever, UT. Here I learned more about mechanics as I had the privilege of working with Quinton Zitting who was always kind to me and taught me whenever he got the chance. I became his little helper and always looked forward to working with him after school. I gained another mentor that I shall always be grateful for in Charlie Zitting. I did many projects with him and always enjoyed his sense of humor and his wisdom. He was a grand friend and a great teacher of life. I graduated from the 7th grade and we then moved to Colorado City, AZ again where I attended the Eith Grade. This was quite a culture shock to me as being in regular school I had girls that I considered friends and we did everything together as well as other boys. Having any relationship with any girl was extremely frowned upon and I had to learn the hard way to not talk to the girls. I believe in the end I learned this lesson well and finally acclimated to this new school and social system that I found myself in. I ended up going to High School here and finished up my education through the Twelveth Grade. I graduated in the Class of 1989.

From here I went to work and did so many jobs that I shall list the ones I remember here but shan't tell but little about each one. I learned to build and repair and install cabinets and did a lot of traveling out of town mainly to Las Vegas, NV and all towns south especially ST. George and the surrounding areas. I even traveled as far as Salt Lake City, UT to install cabinets. I continued working on my own vehicles and learning many trades including Truck Driving, Welding, Concrete work, and many others. I always had a love of computers and had many through the years.

My life really began when I was married by placement marriage during the winter of 1994. My first child was born by the end of the year and my wife and I had 8 children by the time 2009 rolled around. During all of this time we were raising our children in the FLDS Cult. First under Rulon Jeffs and later under Warren Jeffs. We suffered under the reign of Warren more than I care to list here. By the time 2015 rolled around my wife and I were sent away by Lyle Jeffs, Warren's brother and we suffered for 2 years living in Nebraska without our children as we were severely pressured to leave them behind and we would be restored within 2 months supposedly. This was the lie fed to us by Lyle Jeffs when he sent us away. This was a terrible mistake as our 8 children were each terribly abused during these 2 years, the effects of which we still, more than 10 years later, deal with on a daily basis.

So Here we are 10 years later still dealing with the aftermath of the Warren Jeff's regime and trying to survive and build a positive life togethere with our loved ones. Some have moved on and are trying to sort out their own lives Some may never recover and are still struggling immensly from the terrible abuse heaped upon them by members of the Warren Jeffs cult namely Mathew Wall, Lola Fischer and Rachael Fischer. We have reported the abuse and tried to obtain justice, yet so far the perpetrators are at large doing who knows what to those they may be caring for so to speak.

We are Still Writing the Next Chapter

Every new experience adds another chapter. This is where the story continues. We are grateful for each one of our children and we are grateful for all that has been done and is being done for us. Thank you to everyone who has helped and has been kind and has lent a helping hand. May God bless you all!
